CHAPTER 3: RESEARCH METHODOLOGY
Linked to: All Objectives (methodological justification)  |  Est. 10,000–13,000 words

Chapter 3 is the 'how' chapter — it explains and justifies every decision made about how the research was conducted. The cardinal rule is: every methodological choice must be justified, not just described. Do not say 'a questionnaire was used'; say 'a structured questionnaire was used because [reason], which is appropriate for this study because [alignment with objectives].' The chapter must demonstrate that the research design is coherent, rigorous, and capable of generating the evidence needed to answer the research questions and test the hypotheses. Write this chapter as if a highly sceptical examiner is reading it who will challenge every choice.
