Chapter 5 is the most technically complex chapter and the largest empirical chapter. It answers Research Questions 2, 3, 4, and 5 simultaneously. It is divided into three major parts: (A) the identification and validation of key SMMS parameters; (B) the development and validation of the CNN deep learning model for predictive maintenance of concrete structures; and (C) the TISM-based modelling of the structural relationships between SMMS parameters and decision-making criteria. Each part draws on different methods (Delphi + RII for Part A; Python/TensorFlow for Part B; TISM for Part C) but they are unified by the common purpose of generating the analytical inputs for the SMMS framework in Chapter 6.
